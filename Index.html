WHO WE ARE

At O LATEM INTERIOR, we specialize in delivering high-quality tape and joint solutions for residential and commercial spaces. With a focus on precision, durability, and a flawless finish, our team takes pride in transforming drywall surfaces into smooth, paint-ready walls that stand the test of time. Whether it's new construction, renovations, or patch repairs, we ensure a clean, seamless result that enhances the beauty and value of any interior.

OUR SERVICES i

Drywall Taping

We provide professional drywall taping to ensure smooth, seamless walls ready for painting or finishing. Our process includes applying high-quality joint compound, embedding durable tape, and carefully sanding for a flawless result. With attention to detail and precision, we prevent cracks and imperfections, delivering a professional finish. Using top-quality materials and expert techniques, we guarantee a strong, long-lasting surface.

Joint Finishing

Our expert team specializes in professional joint finishing, ensuring a flawless surface for painting or further treatment. We use premium joint compounds and durable tape to create a strong foundation, followed by meticulous sanding for a smooth, even finish. With precision and care, we eliminate imperfections and cracks, delivering a seamless result. By combining top-tier materials with skilled techniques, we guarantee a durable, long-lasting surface that enhances the overall appearance and integrity of your space.

Repair and Patch

We specialize in professional drywall repair and patching, restoring damaged walls to their original smooth finish. Our process includes applying high-quality joint compound, embedding durable tape, and precision sanding to ensure seamless integration with the existing surface. With expert techniques and top-grade materials, we eliminate cracks, holes, and other imperfections, delivering a flawless, long-lasting result. Whether it's minor touch-ups or extensive repairs, we guarantee a strong and polished finish ready for painting or further treatment.

Water Damage Repair

We specialize in professional water damage repair, restoring walls affected by leaks, floods, or excess moisture. Our process includes removing compromised drywall, applying high-quality joint compound, embedding durable tape, and precision sanding to achieve a smooth and seamless finish. With expert techniques and top-grade materials, we eliminate stains, warping, and structural weaknesses to ensure a flawless and long-lasting surface. Whether minor repairs or extensive restoration, our solutions provide durability and a polished result ready for painting or further treatment.

Texture Application

We specialize in professional texture application, adding depth and character to walls with expert techniques. Using high-quality materials, we create stunning finishes such as knockdown, orange peel, and smooth textures to enhance the visual appeal of any space. Our process includes precise application, expert blending, and careful finishing to ensure uniformity and durability. Whether you're aiming for a subtle effect or a bold statement, we deliver a flawless, long-lasting surface ready for painting or further treatment.
